# Retail Sales EDA

## Objective
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to uncover patterns, customer behavior trends, and actionable business insights.

## Tools Used
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
Global Superstore sales data (2011–2014, 51,290 orders), sourced from Kaggle.

## Key Findings
- Sales show consistent year-over-year growth from 2011 to 2014, with a strong seasonal pattern: Q4 is consistently the strongest quarter, Q1 the weakest.
- The Consumer segment leads in total revenue, but this is driven by order volume, not higher spending per order — average order value is nearly identical across all customer segments.
- Furniture is the most heavily discounted category (16.8% average) yet has one of the lowest average profits per order, while Technology — the least discounted category — is the most profitable per order.
- Discount level is moderately negatively correlated with profit (-0.32), suggesting discounting practices are worth reviewing.
